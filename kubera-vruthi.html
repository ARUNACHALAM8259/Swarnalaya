Deposit Scheme

Deposit Scheme

  • Upgrade Your Old Jewellery to New Jewellery.
  • A minimum deposit of 8 grams of 22Kt (916) old gold jewellery is required to the scheme.
  • The scheme will mature after 360 days from the date of enrollment.
  • Enjoy a 75% discount on the value addition based on your weight Deposited.
  • All applicable government taxes will be levied.

For example, if you deposit 10 grams of 22Kt old gold jewellery, you will receive brand new gold jewellery of exactly 10 grams at maturity, with a 75% waiver on value addition (wastage).

Terms, Conditions & FAQs

Account Opening & Enrollment

Who is eligible to enroll in the Scheme ?

The Scheme is open to individual persons only. Entities such as corporations, partnerships, proprietorships, trusts, Hindu Undivided Families (HUF) and Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) are not eligible. Minors may participate through their natural guardians.

What is the minimum enrollment Amount in the Scheme ?

The minimum deposit is 8 grams of 22Kt (916) old gold jewellery.

What is the Scheme Book and how should it be handled ?

The Scheme Book is a record book issued at enrollment to record the deposited old gold details. It must be presented at maturity and kept safe. A duplicate Scheme Book can be issued after the account holder files a police report (FIR) and provides an indemnity letter on Rs.200/- stamp paper along with photo ID and address proof.

What happens if there are discrepancies in my Scheme Book ?

Any discrepancies must be reported immediately in writing to Swarnalaya management for verification and rectification.

What if I change my contact or address details ?

Account holders must notify Swarnalaya immediately with valid photo ID and address proof to update system contact information.

Can I transfer my enrollment to another scheme or convert it to another currency ?

No. Schemes are non-transferable and conversion between different schemes is not permitted.

How are the installments accumulated ?

For Kubera Vruthi, this is a one-time old gold deposit scheme, so there are no installments. The net 22Kt gold weight is locked at enrollment.

What is the scheme benefit ?

Upon maturity (360 days), you can purchase new 22Kt gold jewellery of equivalent weight with a 75% discount on the value addition (wastage).

Monthly Payments

How do I make monthly payments, and what documentation do I need ?

Payments must be made monthly, on or before the due date, via cash, card, UPI, or online methods. Each payment must be documented by requesting a computerized receipt or by collecting an updated receipt from the Store. The Scheme Book must be updated with each payment. (Note: As Kubera Vruthi is a one-time old gold deposit scheme, standard monthly payments are not required unless adding separate custom transactions).

Can I make all or any monthly payments in advance ?

As a gold deposit scheme, the entire old gold deposit is made at the time of joining. Additional gold deposits cannot be made in monthly advance.

How is monthly installment date calculated ?

There are no monthly installments for this scheme; the maturity date is calculated as 360 days from the date of the initial gold deposit.

Is monthly installment Amount fixed?

This scheme has no monthly installments. The deposited gold weight is recorded and fixed at enrollment.

What if online transaction is failed ?

Gold deposits are made in person at the showroom. Online payment queries do not apply to the initial physical deposit.

Are there any payment methods Swarnalaya does not accept?

For deposits under this scheme, Swarnalaya accepts 22Kt (916) old gold jewellery. Cash, card, and other payments are not accepted as deposits under this scheme.

Am I responsible for ensuring payments are made on time?

Yes, the account holder is responsible for initiating the scheme at the store on time with their physical gold deposit.

What happens if I miss a payment?

As there are no monthly installments, there is no risk of missing payments. The scheme matures 360 days from the initial deposit date.

Scheme Redemption

How is the maturity date determined ?

The Maturity date falls on the 360th day from the date of joining.

How do I redeem my installments for jewellery ?

Account holders must present the original Scheme Book along with valid photo ID and address proof at the Swarnalaya store to purchase jewellery against the accumulated weight.

What kind of products is eligible to purchase under this Scheme ?

You can purchase brand new 22Kt gold jewellery under this scheme.

What additional charges apply when redeeming the Scheme for jewellery ?

Making charges, wastage (value addition - VA), and government taxes (GST) on the chosen jewellery are payable by the customer.

Can I redeem part of my Scheme account or receive a refund ?

No. Partial redemptions are not permitted. Under no circumstances will cash refunds or returning of the original old gold jewellery be issued.

What happens if I close my Scheme account prematurely ?

In case of premature closure, you can purchase jewellery for the net weight of gold deposited, but you will not receive the 75% value addition discount benefit.

Can I club both the scheme benefits and the Prevailing Offers / Discounts on redemption of the scheme ?

The scheme cannot be clubbed with other promotional discounts or offers unless explicitly permitted by Swarnalaya management at redemption.
